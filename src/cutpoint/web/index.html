              Export & Assembly Options
              Clips to Export Exports the moments in the sequence, in its order and with your trims. Lets Cutpoint pick the best-scoring moments of the analysis (clips --top, --ids, --max-total, --order); the sequence is not used.
              Exports the N best-scoring moments (--top). Blank: the style's count. Caps the video's length: the clips' total in seconds, keeping the best-scoring moments that fit (--max-total). Blank: no cap. Exports exactly these candidates of highlights.json, by the ids on the moment cards such as h001 h004, instead of the top moments (--ids). The clips' playback order (--order): Style uses the style's order; Story puts the second-best first and the best last.
              Exports the clips as 9:16 vertical video (1080x1920, center crop) for Shorts, Reels and TikTok. Transcribes the final video and burns its words onto the picture as captions. Moves in and out points that fall mid-word to the start or end of the sentence instead of using your exact trims (--speech-aware), in Export clips and Extend export. Lets a clip that ends on speech run on until the reaction settles (--smart-tail). Evens the final video to a standard streaming loudness (-14 LUFS). Keeps the audio transition matched to the video transition: Cut with Cut, Crossfade with Fade.
              How the picture changes from one clip to the next: a hard cut or a short fade. How the sound changes from one clip to the next: a hard cut or a short crossfade, which needs the Fade video transition.
              Cut Timing
              The most seconds Speech-aware cuts may move an end later to finish a sentence (--speech-max-extend). Blank: 8. The most seconds Speech-aware cuts may move a start earlier to catch a sentence's start (--speech-max-lead). Blank: 5. Seconds a smart tail always keeps after the last word (--tail-min). Blank: the style's, 1.5 for most. The most seconds a smart tail adds after the last word (--tail-max). Blank: the style's, 5 for most. Extends a clip to cover a payoff such as a kill, banner or laughter that starts soon after the speech (--event-tails). Auto: on when the project has events.json. Needs Smart tails for the sequence's moments. A payoff must start within this many seconds after the speech (--event-window). Blank: 10. Seconds of padding added before every clip, in Export clips (--extend-start) and Extend export (--start). Blank: none. Seconds of padding added after every clip, in Export clips (--extend-end) and Extend export (--end). Blank: none.
              Compress Audio Peaks
              NoneLightMax
              Evens out loud peaks such as shouts and explosions and levels the clips, in Export clips and Assemble.
              Color
              OffAutoUnderwater
              Corrects dull, washed-out or tinted color per clip, in Export clips and One-Click Edit (--color). Auto evens levels, white balance and vibrance; Underwater also restores red. Clips that already look good, log footage and 360 clips stay as they are. Starts at Off for gaming footage.
              100% How much of the color correction to apply (--color-strength); 100% is the full correction.

              Re-exports the latest export's clips into a new folder with the Extend start and end padding and the speech settings (cutpoint extend).
              One-Click Edit
              What cutpoint edit makes from the analysis, in one run: the best moments as clips, then the final video (--kind). Highlights: up to 3 minutes, chronological, with fades. Montage: up to 60 seconds, best first, with fades. Clips: the 10 best, hard cuts. Funny moments: 8, chronological, with burned-in captions. Uses Vertical 9:16 and Compress Audio Peaks.

              Runs the preset: analyzes again if the footage changed, exports the clips to the clips folder and assembles final.mp4. The sequence is not used.
