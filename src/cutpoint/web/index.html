              Export & Assembly Options
              Clips to Export Exports the moments in the sequence, in its order and with your trims. Lets Cutpoint pick the best-scoring moments of the analysis (clips --top, --ids, --max-total, --order); the sequence is not used.
              Exports the N best-scoring moments (--top). Blank: the style's count. Caps the video's length: the clips' total in seconds, keeping the best-scoring moments that fit (--max-total). Blank: no cap. Exports exactly these candidates of highlights.json, by the ids on the moment cards (shown with Settings > Verbose moment labels) such as h001 h004, instead of the top moments (--ids). The clips' playback order (--order): Style uses the style's order; Story puts the second-best first and the best last.
              Exports the clips as 9:16 vertical video (1080x1920, center crop) for Shorts, Reels and TikTok. Transcribes the final video and burns its words onto the picture as captions. Moves in and out points that fall mid-word to the start or end of the sentence instead of using your exact trims (--speech-aware), in Export clips and Extend export. Lets a clip that ends on speech run on until the reaction settles (--smart-tail). Evens the final video to a standard streaming loudness (-14 LUFS). Keeps the audio transition matched to the video transition: Cut with Cut, Crossfade with Fade. Not used with a Final Cut or OpenTimelineIO output: a timeline export has no audio processing or burned-in captions.
              What Assemble writes (assemble --format): the final video, or a timeline for Final Cut Pro or DaVinci Resolve (fcpxml) or OpenTimelineIO tools (otio) that uses the exported clips as they are, with the transitions, in about a second. Keep the clips folder for a timeline. How the picture changes from one clip to the next: a hard cut or a short fade. How the sound changes from one clip to the next: a hard cut or a short crossfade, which needs the Fade video transition. Seconds each Fade between two clips lasts, in Assemble (assemble --fade-dur). Needs the Fade video transition, or a join set to Fade in Cut mode. Blank: 0.25. Seconds of audio fade in and out Assemble adds to clips exported without one, such as with Clip audio fade 0 (assemble --audio-fade; 0 = off). With the Crossfade audio transition, used only for joins set to Cut in Cut mode and the video's two ends. Blank: 0.25. The Whisper model that transcribes Burn in captions (assemble --model). Default: medium with an NVIDIA GPU, small on the CPU; large-v3 is the most accurate and the slowest.
              Cut Timing
              The most seconds Speech-aware cuts may move an end later to finish a sentence (--speech-max-extend). Blank: 8. The most seconds Speech-aware cuts may move a start earlier to catch a sentence's start (--speech-max-lead). Blank: 5. Seconds a smart tail always keeps after the last word (--tail-min). Blank: the style's, 1.5 for most. The most seconds a smart tail adds after the last word (--tail-max). Blank: the style's, 5 for most. Extends a clip to cover a payoff such as a kill, banner or laughter that starts soon after the speech (--event-tails). Auto: on when the project has events.json. Needs Smart tails for the sequence's moments. A payoff must start within this many seconds after the speech (--event-window). Blank: 10. Seconds of padding added before every clip, in Export clips (--extend-start) and Extend export (--start). Blank: none. Seconds of padding added after every clip, in Export clips (--extend-end) and Extend export (--end). Blank: none.
              Clip Picture & Audio
              Crops each clip's picture before scaling, in Export clips (clips --crop). Default: the project's per-source crop, else none. Auto finds black borders per source; Custom takes a box in pixels. The crop box in source pixels: width:height:x:y from the top-left corner, such as 1920:800:0:140 (clips --crop W:H:X:Y). Each clip's resolution in even pixels, width x height, such as 1280x720, in Export clips (clips --size). Not used with Vertical 9:16. Blank: the largest source's, at most 1080p. Seconds of audio fade in and out baked into every clip, in Export clips (clips --audio-fade; 0 = off). Blank: 0.25.
              Compress Audio Peaks
              NoneLightMax
              Evens out loud peaks such as shouts and explosions and levels the clips, in Export clips and Assemble.
              Color
              OffAutoUnderwater
              Corrects dull, washed-out or tinted color per clip, in Export clips and One-Click Edit (--color). Auto evens levels, white balance and vibrance; Underwater also restores red. Clips that already look good, log footage and 360 clips stay as they are. Starts at Off for gaming footage.
              100% How much of the color correction to apply (--color-strength); 100% is the full correction.

              Re-exports the latest export's clips into a new folder with the Extend start and end padding and the speech settings (cutpoint extend).
              One-Click Edit Experimental

              Picks and joins moments without your review. Use the sequence for a finished edit.

              What cutpoint edit makes from the analysis, in one run: the best moments as clips, then the final video (--kind). Highlights: up to 3 minutes, chronological, with fades. Montage: up to 60 seconds, best first, with fades. Clips: the 10 best, hard cuts. Funny moments: 8, chronological, with burned-in captions. Uses Vertical 9:16 and Compress Audio Peaks.

              Runs the preset: analyzes again if the footage changed, exports the clips to the clips folder and assembles final.mp4. The sequence is not used.

                Connected accounts

                Subscriptions are managed by CLIProxyAPI. Cutpoint starts the proxy when needed and stops it when the UI closes. Use at your own risk.

                More

                Credentials use the OS keyring; the proxy needs private plaintext working files while running. A guardian handles CLI hard kills; keyring-write failures use encrypted recovery. A host crash or failed recovery write can leave private files. Antigravity sign-in may provision a free-tier project if your account has none.
